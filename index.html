Hints and lab instructions

Finish the program, or leave only idle event listeners while all motors stay stopped on the goal for 0.3 seconds. Stop/Reset interrupts an attempt.

Practice robot: 56 mm wheels, 112 mm spacing, drive A/B, color C and distance D. Use the block editor and Run controls. Import, shared saves and robot/field changes are disabled during practice.

    Build a program, then press Run.

    20-run reliability trials

    An optional sensitivity experiment for this simple scene, not a mission score or a prediction of real LEGO reliability. No “normal LEGO” preset is assumed. Zero settings repeat the nominal scene. Wheel parameters change effective ground travel per encoder degree, not unregulated motor speed.

    Errors are independent uniform draws within ±bounds, held constant for each run. Fixed wheel biases apply to every run. This models a chosen stress case; motor regulation may compensate real motor differences.

    Choose the conditions to explore. Results include seed and exact settings.

      Teaching debugger

      Pause freezes simulation time. From idle, Step starts a new run paused. One step advances every active stack by 20 ms; waits and moves need multiple steps. Existing block highlights and variables follow all stacks. The small magenta disk shows the approximate 8 mm sensing footprint (not measured SPIKE optics); the larger robot circle only locates the sensor; the ray shows distance sensing.

      Motor degrees count shaft rotation. Chassis heading measures the robot's direction. They are not interchangeable.
